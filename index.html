MVP+ Scoring Formula
MVP+  =  ( (PFF Grade × 2)  +  ( (Wins / GP) × 125 )  +  ( (Pass TD / GP) × 32 )  +  ( (Rush TD / GP) × 16 )  ) / 3.75  +  Team Performance Bonus  −  Missed Games Penalty
PFF Grade ×2
Pro Football Focus game grade (0–100). Doubled to heavily weight overall efficiency, accuracy, and decision-making quality.
(Wins / GP) × 125 ~33%
Win rate scaled to games played. Normalizes for missed games. The single largest predictor — voters reward winning QBs above all else.
(Pass TD / GP) × 32 ~19%
Per-game passing touchdown rate. Volume stat that drives public visibility. Scaled by 32 to put it on the same magnitude as the other components.
(Rush TD / GP) × 16 ~10%
Per-game rushing touchdown rate at half the weight of pass TDs. Rewards dual-threat QBs like Lamar Jackson (7 rush TD) and Josh Allen (14 rush TD in 2025).
÷ 3.75 normalization
Divides the bracketed sum by 3.75 to bring the raw total down to a readable 0–120 range. Without this, scores would routinely exceed 350+.
Bonuses & Penalties
Team Performance Bonus (0–3.6 pts) rewards sustained excellence. Missed Games Penalty docks players who miss significant time through injury or suspension.
